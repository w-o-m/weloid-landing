# 1. Executive Summary

Weloid is a software engineering and technology company that builds software, rescues troubled products, investigates complex technical problems, provides specialist engineering teams, and develops its own SaaS products.

Weloid exists for companies that need more than generic outsourced developers.

A customer may come to Weloid because:

* They have an idea and need it built.
* They have software that is unfinished.
* Their previous development company disappeared.
* Their application works but is becoming impossible to maintain.
* They built an application using AI and do not know whether the code is production-ready.
* Their engineering team cannot determine why a system is failing.
* They need an independent code or architecture audit.
* They need developers but do not want to recruit an entire team.
* They need a temporary satellite engineering team.
* They need specialists for a particular technology or problem.
* They need technical due diligence before buying or investing in a company.
* They need to modernize an old system.
* They need help scaling infrastructure or engineering processes.
* They need fractional technical leadership.
* They need a custom internal system or SaaS platform.
* They need experienced engineers to take ownership of a difficult technical problem.

Weloid's fundamental promise is:

> **We build, fix, investigate and scale software.**

Weloid should not position itself as a cheap outsourcing company.

It should position itself as an engineering company that organizations call when software matters.
